How 402Signal routes a request

A route starts with what you need and any constraints you want to enforce.

1 Discover
Find candidates across supported x402 discovery sources.
2 Check
Call candidate endpoints and read the payment requirements they return now.
3 Apply constraints
Apply your network, price, latency, and invocation constraints.
4 Select
Get the best qualifying route, or a typed reason nothing matched.
5 Execute
Your application keeps control of its wallet, signs the seller payment, and calls the selected service.
When nothing matches
If nothing meets your constraints, 402Signal returns a typed miss instead of guessing.
