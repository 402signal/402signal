Route before you spend

POST /route with what you need and any constraints.

An unpaid request returns HTTP 402 for the $0.01 USDC routing fee. After that payment is verified, 402Signal checks candidates and returns the best qualifying route or a typed miss.

402Signal recommends the route. Your application keeps control of its wallet, signs the selected service's payment, and calls the service.

Request

{"need":"erc20 token balance","prefer_network":"base"}

Routing fee

A routing check costs $0.01 USDC.

The routing fee pays 402Signal. Any payment required by the selected service is separate and remains under the caller's control.
