From finding an API to paying it

One check before choosing. Another before signing.

x402 lets software pay for APIs one request at a time. A listed offer can change, so 402Signal checks the terms the service returns now.

  1. 1

    Find an API

    Describe what your agent needs or supply a URL. 402Signal searches supported sources across three networks.

  2. 2

    Check it against your rules

    It calls candidates, reads their payment offers, and applies your price, network, response-time and request-format requirements. You get one qualifying route or a reason to stop.

  3. 3

    Verify the offer before signing

    With optional v4 protection integrated into your app, a local guard compares the fresh seller offer and exact request with signed evidence. Changed terms or expired evidence stop the handoff to payment code. Your wallet still validates and signs the actual transaction.

Your agent finds a weather API for 2 cents.

402Signal checks whether it responds with a usable offer within your budget. If the seller then asks for 3 cents or changes the payment recipient, the v4 guard rejects the changed offer before handing it to your payment code.

A successful check verifies the observed offer. It does not guarantee delivery or the quality of the weather data.

PQ Trust

A record of what was checked.

A payment receipt shows that money moved. 402Signal's verification record preserves the request, observed terms and decision evidence. If someone edits your saved record later, verification against the public log fails.

402Signal records routing evidence in an append-only Merkle log. It publishes a fingerprint of the evidence, not its private contents. V4 requires a signed log receipt immediately. Cumulative checkpoints are anchored on Algorand MainNet with native Falcon-1024 authorization. A pending record is not yet confirmed on-chain.

To verify a decision later, securely keep the verification record. Private replay outcomes can retain evidence, but are not a recovery service.

Falcon-1024 authorizes the checkpoint transaction. It does not make seller payments or API responses post-quantum secure.
